# Globall Cloud Roadmap

## Executive summary
Globall Cloud already has a strong delivery-operations foundation across shipment tracking, warehouse intake, driver workflows, staff tooling, notification flows, and core payments. The next stage is to convert the platform from a logistics website into a complete enterprise logistics operating system.

## What is already strong
- Public tracking and customer-facing shipment visibility
- Customer and driver portals
- Warehouse receipts and route state tracking
- Notification and event layers
- Control plane and operational workflows
- Payment flows for QI Card and FIB
- Staff accounts, audit logs, and admin controls

## Major gaps to close
### 1) Unified Enterprise Dashboard
The project currently exposes separate experiences for customer, driver, control plane, and admin views. These should converge into one enterprise operating layer:

- Operations
- Warehouse
- CRM
- Finance
- HR
- Analytics
- AI Center

### 2) Finance ERP
Current payment support is present, but accounting and ERP layers are still incomplete:

- General Ledger
- Expenses
- Profit & Loss
- Cashflow
- Branch accounting
- Payroll

### 3) HR system
The repo has staff accounts and admin views, but the HR layer is incomplete:

- Attendance
- Leave management
- Payroll
- Staff KPI dashboards

### 4) Fleet management
There is strong driver workflow coverage, but fleet operations still need complete management support:

- Vehicles
- Fuel tracking
- Service schedule
- Vehicle costs

### 5) BI and analytics
The repo includes dashboards, but the enterprise analytics capability still needs expansion:

- Executive reports
- Revenue forecast
- Branch comparison
- KPI center

### 6) AI layer
No dedicated AI layer is visible in the repository documentation yet:

- ETA prediction
- OCR document processing
- AI support agent
- Smart pricing
- Demand forecast

### 7) Native mobile delivery
The experience is mobile-first, but full mobile product delivery is still not complete:

- Android app
- iPhone app
- Push notifications
- Offline sync

## Proposed delivery phases

### Phase 0 — Governance and scope alignment
- Confirm investor/internal business scope
- Define enterprise modules and ownership
- Create roadmap, issues, and milestones
- Align design system and module boundaries

### Phase 1 — Enterprise OS + finance foundation
- Unified enterprise shell and role-based navigation
- General ledger + journal posting foundation
- Basic accounting reports and branch comparison
- RBAC and access matrix

### Phase 2 — HR + fleet modules
- Staff management and attendance
- Leave and payroll flows
- Vehicle registry and maintenance scheduling
- Fuel and cost tracking

### Phase 3 — BI + AI + mobile
- KPI center and executive dashboards
- Forecasting, OCR, pricing, and support automation
- Native app delivery and notifications

## Recommended priority order
1. Unified enterprise dashboard
2. Finance ERP core
3. HR system
4. Fleet management
5. BI analytics
6. AI center
7. Mobile apps

## Target outcome
Once the above modules are added, the project can move beyond a logistics website and become a true Enterprise Logistics Operating System comparable to the operating model used by advanced regional and global logistics providers.
